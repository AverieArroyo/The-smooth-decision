01 / THE TRADEOFF
W

Waxing is lighter today.

Lower upfront cost, but hair grows back and appointments continue indefinitely.

L

Laser invests in later.

A higher upfront cost with the goal of long-term hair reduction, potentially reducing the ongoing need for hair removal.

Laser does not guarantee every hair will be permanently gone. Results vary, and future maintenance or touch-ups may still be needed.
